# BME2315_Module3
Computational Drug Discovery--- taught by Dr. Saucerman

Description: we are working as a Quantitative Systems Pharmacologist at the UVA Manning Institute for Biotechnology. Our overall goal is to help develop a GLP1 agonist drug for weight loss, branded Cavalitide, that will compete with semaglutide (Ozempic or Wegovy). Our project is composed of three tasks:
* Task 1: Fit a receptor binding (pharmacodynamics) model for Cavalitide
* Task 2: Simulate the pharmacokinetics of Cavalitide treatment
* Task 3: Simulate a combined pharmacokinetics-pharmacodynamics (PKPD) model that predicts GLP1 receptor activity during treatment.
